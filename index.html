Election Feed Configuration

Results come only from official election offices: the Colorado Secretary of State and Arapahoe County Clarity feeds, plus each state’s own results feed for the National tab. A scheduled job refreshes results.json and national_results.json. Counts are unofficial until certified. Colorado doesn’t “call” races, so contests show as leaning until you confirm them.
